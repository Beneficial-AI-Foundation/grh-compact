Gap 1: The first obstruction is that an admissible geometric datum is undefined. The sketch specifies no lattice, lift map, height, or conditions relating the listed parameters to a covolume. It also gives no definition of either prime-error function. Consequently there is no specified upper or lower covolume bound to compare, and the claimed cancellation cannot be checked.
Gap 2: The admissibility conditions are missing, so there is no way to verify that a proposed datum realizes m, b, f, H, and T simultaneously. The prime-error functions and their transforms are also undefined, and no estimate is supplied that yields a point in the stated interval where the indicated combination is at most x^(-1000000). The interval alone gives no such bound.

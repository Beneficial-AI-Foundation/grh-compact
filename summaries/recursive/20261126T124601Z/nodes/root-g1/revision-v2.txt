Gap 1: The required spacing between thick places is unspecified. The sketch also gives no admissibility conditions relating s, the ranks, the auxiliary primes, and the integral forms to the weights. The count H ≥ T/log b gives no spacing or admissibility conclusion, so the construction cannot be checked for any u or uniformly across the interval.
Gap 2: The sketch gives no definitions of the target forms, theta lifts, their height h, or the prime-error functions R_{d,N}, so neither the covolume bound nor the weighted prime-sum identity can be verified. Under multiplication of residue classes, translating a top by an integer congruent to 1 modulo M puts it among the tops. Membership in the chosen thick subset requires a further check. The congruence alone establishes the claimed equivalence for all tops only if every top is thick.
Gap 3: The required inequality is explicit, but the sketch supplies no congruence conditions for the twists or identities expressing the volume ratios in terms of absolute values of a nonzero field element. Those data are needed to apply the product formula. The scalar conditions of Gap 2 alone permit n = D = 1, δ = 0, h = 4L, and mQ_u = 5L, which give V = 0 and D(h−3L) = L > 0.

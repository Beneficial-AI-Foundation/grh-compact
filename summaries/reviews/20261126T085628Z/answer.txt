**Gap 1 — Unproved.** The first obstruction is that “admissible geometric datum” is undefined in the supplied material. In particular, there is no definition of the lattice of lifts or of the height used to measure them. The formulas for \(\mathcal B\) and \(\mathrm{rem}\) supply no covolume or index estimates. Thus there is no stated basis for constructing short lifts or verifying the claimed cancellation of the main height.

**Gap 2 — Unproved.** The first obstruction is the same undefined admissibility condition, which prevents verifying any proposed construction for a given \(x\). The last estimate has a separate missing input. No prime-error transform or estimate relating its poles to values of \(R_M(x_1)-(T/H)R_1(x_1)\) in the specified window is supplied. The allowed criterion takes an eventual prime-error bound as a hypothesis, so it cannot supply that estimate.

**The deduction from both gaps is valid.** Fix \(q,\chi,\rho\) as in the target, excluding the principal case at \(\rho=1\). Set \(n=9240q\), so \(M=2n\) and \(a=n-1\). Since \(n\) is divisible by \(8\), \(a\equiv7\pmod8\) and \(\gcd(a,2n)=\gcd(n-1,2)=1\). Every prime \(d\equiv a\pmod M\) therefore has the criterion’s required properties. Also \(d+1\equiv n\pmod{2n}\), which gives \(M\mid2(d+1)\) and \(M\nmid d+1\) as required by the gaps.

Writing \(t=\min(\Re\rho,1)-1/2\), we have \(0<t\le1/2\). Hence \(0<\varepsilon=t/51\le1/102<1/100\) and
\[
\tfrac12<\beta=\tfrac12+\tfrac8{51}t<\tfrac12+t\le\Re\rho.
\]
For each fixed prime \(d\) in the class, Gap 2 would give a datum for every sufficiently large \(x\). Applying Gap 1 to that datum and dividing by \(mAH>0\) would give
\[
R_M(x)\le \frac{f(\log b)^5}{mH}+x^{-10^6}
\le \varphi(M)x^{6\varepsilon-1/2}+x^{-10^6}.
\]
After division by \(x^{\beta-1}=x^{8\varepsilon-1/2}\), the upper bound is
\[
\varphi(M)x^{-2\varepsilon}+x^{1/2-8\varepsilon-10^6},
\]
which tends to zero. It is therefore at most \(1\) eventually. The threshold may depend on \(d\), as the criterion requires an eventual bound for each prime separately.

The criterion would then give nonvanishing at \(\rho\) for the entire \(L\)-function of the character induced to \(M\). Induction preserves whether the character is principal. At the points under consideration, removal of the principal pole does not change the zero set, since the factor \(s-1\) is nonzero away from \(1\). The finite Euler factors for the change of modulus are also nonzero there. This yields the target conditional on both gaps.

**Whole argument — does not close.** Neither gap is proved from the supplied material, so the prime-error hypothesis of the allowed criterion remains unestablished.
